# CivicLens 🌍

### Turning Community Problems into Actionable Reports

CivicLens is an AI-powered civic problem-solving platform designed to make reporting public issues simpler, smarter, and more accessible. Built with Google Gemini AI, it helps people transform photographs and descriptions of everyday civic problems into structured reports and ready-to-send complaints.

## 🚨 The Problem

Issues such as potholes, overflowing garbage, water leaks, and broken streetlights affect communities every day. However, identifying the right department, describing the problem clearly, and preparing a formal complaint can be confusing and time-consuming.

## 💡 Our Solution

CivicLens bridges the gap between noticing a problem and taking action by combining AI-powered analysis with community-driven reporting.

### ✨ Key Features

- **AI-Powered Issue Analysis:** Uses Google Gemini AI to analyze submitted photographs or descriptions of civic problems.
- **Smart Urgency Assessment:** Suggests an urgency level and explains the potential impact of an issue.
- **Complaint Generation:** Creates clear, editable complaint drafts that users can copy and submit through appropriate channels.
- **Community Reporting Feed:** Brings civic reports together in one place.
- **Issue Confirmation:** Allows users to confirm that an existing reported issue affects them too.
- **Status Tracking:** Displays report statuses such as Open, In Progress, and Resolved.
- **Search and Filters:** Helps users explore reports by category and urgency.
- **Responsive Interface:** Designed to work across desktop and mobile devices.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Report:** Upload a photograph or describe a public issue.
2. **Analyze:** Gemini AI identifies the issue and generates an assessment.
3. **Prepare:** Review the suggested urgency, department, and complaint draft.
4. **Share:** Add the report to the community feed and discover related issues.
5. **Act:** Copy the complaint and submit it through an appropriate reporting channel.

## 🧠 Technology

- **Google Gemini AI** — AI-powered image and text analysis.
- **Web technologies** — Responsive user interface and interactive reporting workflow.
- **Local persistence** — Stores demonstration reports and confirmations in the browser.

*The exact implementation depends on the technologies generated and configured in the project.*

## 🎯 Vision

Our vision is to make civic participation easier by helping people communicate public problems clearly and take informed next steps.

CivicLens is designed to demonstrate how AI can support communities through practical, accessible technology.

## ⚠️ Current Scope

CivicLens is a prototype. AI-generated assessments and department suggestions may require verification. Reports and statuses in the demo are not necessarily connected to official authorities, and the application does not claim to submit complaints automatically.

## 🚀 Built For

A Google AI Studio hackathon project exploring how generative AI can help solve everyday community problems.
